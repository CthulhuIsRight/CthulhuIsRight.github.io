Navy service

Training, Great Lakes and Dam Neck (Nov 2017 to Aug 2019)

Basic training, then Fire Controlman “A” school, the Phalanx CIWS technical course, and the MK 15 Block 1B Baseline 2 differential course. Hands-on work with oscilloscopes, multimeters, circuit diagrams, and electrical troubleshooting.

Fire Controlman Second Class, USS Nimitz (2019 to Mar 2023)

Operated and maintained the Phalanx close-in weapon system (CIWS). Qualified Enlisted Surface Warfare Specialist (ESWS) in 2021.

  • Diagnosed and corrected seven CIWS casualties (system failures), across 432 hours of preventive and corrective maintenance.
  • Stood 729 hours of watch in the Combat Systems Maintenance Center, running the CIWS Local Control System console on six to eight hour watches.
  • Took part in 15 live-fire pre-action calibrations.
  • Helped maintain continuous 360 degree anti-air warfare coverage; the ship scored 100% on its Combat Systems Readiness Assessment.

Counter-UAS operator, Naval Base Kitsap-Bremerton (May 2023 to Apr 2024)

Ran the CORIAN counter-drone system defending restricted airspace over the base.

  • Installed, operated, and maintained the system and its servers.
  • Applied rules of engagement to decide when electronic attack against a drone threat was warranted, and carried out four successful drone exercises.
  • Reported to the watch commander, the base anti-terrorism force, and the Federal Communications Commission.

Weapon and sensor systems have to behave predictably, fail safely, and leave a record of what they did. Industrial control systems carry the same requirements, and that's the work I'm aiming at.
